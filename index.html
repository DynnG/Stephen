About Stephen

Thoughtful design.
Built for your space.

I turn ideas into furniture and cabinetry that bring together beauty, strength, and everyday function. What drives me is seeing a concept take shape, from the first sketch and careful measurements to the final piece.

My strengths in joinery, space planning, and refined furniture design help me make the most of each material and every inch of your space. With precise construction and attention to detail, I create pieces designed to fit your needs, complement your surroundings, and stand up to everyday use.
